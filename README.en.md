## 🔧 Development & Programming

### GitHub

- **GitHub Copilot**: Free for students

### JetBrains
- **IntelliJ IDEA**: Free license for students and educators
- **PyCharm**: Free license for students and educators
- **WebStorm**: Free license for students and educators
- **CLion**: Free license for students and educators
- **Eligibility**: Students and educators at accredited institutions

### Microsoft
- **Visual Studio**: Free license for students
- **Azure**: Free credits for students
- **Office 365 Education**: Free for students and educators
- **Office 365**: Student discount available

## 🎵 Music & Entertainment

### Spotify
- **Premium Student**: Approximately 50% off
- **Eligibility**: University, junior college, graduate school, vocational school, technical college students
- **Note**: Not available for high school students and below

### YouTube Music
- **Student Plan**: Lower price than regular plan
- **Verification**: Student verification required

### Apple Music
- **Student Plan**: Approximately 50% off
- **Eligibility**: University and graduate school students

### Amazon Music
- **Student Plan**: Special price for Prime Student members

### AWA
- **Student Plan**: Available from junior high school students

- **Rakuten Music**: Student plan available
- **TOWER RECORDS MUSIC**: Student discount available

- **Note**: No official student discount currently available in Japan

### U-NEXT

## 🚆 Transportation

### JR Commuter Pass
- **Discount Rate**: Approximately 50% off (varies by route)
- **Eligibility**: Junior high, high school, university, vocational school students
- **Required Documents**: Student ID, commuter pass certificate

### Private Railway & Subway Commuter Pass
- **Discount Rate**: Approximately 30-50% off (varies by line)
- **Eligibility**: Students
- **Required Documents**: Student ID, commuter pass certificate

### Airline Tickets (ANA・JAL)
- **Student Discount**: Sky Mate (JAL), Smart U25 (ANA)
- **Discount Rate**: Up to approximately 50% off
- **Eligibility**: Ages 12-25 (varies by service)

### Highway Bus
- **Student Discount**: Student discounts at many bus companies
- **Discount Rate**: Approximately 10-20%
- **Eligibility**: Student ID required
